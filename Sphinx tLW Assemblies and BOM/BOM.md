# Sphinx Toolhead — Hardware BOM

Fasteners and inserts for Sphinx tLW. The **common hardware** is the same for the Voron and Monolith versions. Add **one hotend kit** for the hotend you're running.

All screws are **button head (BHCS)** unless noted.

## Hotend kits

Pick the row for your hotend and add it to the common totals.

| Hotend | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---:|
| Tricorn | M3x12 BHCS | 4 |
| Goliath | M2.5x8 BHCS | 4 |
| CHC XL | M2.5x8 BHCS | 4 |
| Rapido UHF / RapidoX | M2.5x10 BHCS | 4 |
| Dragon Ace | M2.5x10 BHCS | 4 |
| Dragon Ace Volcano (DAV) | M2.5x10 BHCS | 4 |

> **Tricorn note:** Tricorn uses M3x12, the same size as the main body, so a Tricorn build needs **14× M3x12** in total.

Every complete build is **64 pieces**: 60 common + 4 hotend.
